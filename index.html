Reconstitution & Dosing Guide

Every injectable in the True Peptide Labs catalog, as listed on 9 Oct 2026. All draws are for a U-100 insulin syringe (100 units = 1 mL).

Mixing, every vial

  1. Run the bac water slowly down the inside wall of the vial.
  2. Swirl gently. Don't shake.
  3. Store in the fridge and use within about 30 days.
